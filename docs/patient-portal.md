# Patient Portal

The patient portal is the patient-facing area for account access, intake forms, orders, invoices, subscriptions, appointments, messages, and profile updates.

## Patient Workflows

- Complete assigned or required intake forms
- View orders and subscription status
- Pay invoices
- Manage profile and payment information
- Review appointments and messages

## Documentation Notes

Use fake sample patients and fake screenshots only. Do not publish protected health information in this public docs site.
